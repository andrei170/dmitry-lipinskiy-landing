Pillar One · Accounting and Job Costing

Where the money actually goes

"You cannot manage a roofing company you cannot measure. Gross margin per job is the starting number."

QuickBooks setup and job costing basics. Gross margin targets by job type and revenue band. Overhead allocation. Material cost tracking and supplier terms. Labor rate and sub vs. employee decision. Change orders and scope creep recovery. The numbers most roofing company owners never learned to read.

Pillar Two · Marketing and Lead Generation

Getting off the referral dependency

"Referral-only is a ceiling. It caps at the size of your personal network. Marketing builds the floor."

Cost per lead by channel: paid, organic, referral, and repeat. Lead source tracking. Google and Meta basics for roofing companies. Referral system mechanics for contractors who want more from the same happy customers. Content strategy for contractors who want to build an authority platform in their market. Review generation and online reputation management.

Pillar Three · Production and Operations

The shop floor when it scales

"The crew that works at 5 trucks does not work at 10. Operations has to be rebuilt at every tier."

Production schedule management. Crew deployment and daily standup cadence. Field supervisor role definition and hire timing. Sub vs. employee economics by revenue tier. Warranty call and callback protocols. Admin and office manager responsibilities by company size. The operations infrastructure that lets you step off the truck and run the business.

Pillar Four · Admin and Business Infrastructure

The back office that holds the whole thing together

"Admin is not overhead. It is the system that keeps the other three pillars from falling apart."

Hiring and onboarding processes. Contract and scope documentation. Insurance, licensing, and compliance basics by state. Customer communication SOPs. Payment collections and invoice management. The admin infrastructure that a roofing company needs before it can scale without the owner running everything.

Before you join

Common questions,
answered directly.

How is Roofing School different from the YouTube channel?

The YouTube channel shows you what the four business problems look like. Roofing School walks you through fixing them inside your actual company. Monthly training modules, weekly group calls where your specific situation goes on the table, and a community of operators at different revenue tiers. The channel is the framework. The School is the install.

Who is this for? Is it for new companies or established operators?

Both. If you are at $0-$500K, the accounting and ops foundation is where you start: job costing, QuickBooks basics, gross margin targets. If you are at $500K-$3M, the marketing and hiring systems are the ceiling to break. If you are past $3M, the community and the calls give you peers at your level who have solved problems you have not hit yet. The monthly modules rotate across all four pillars, so every tier gets something relevant every month.

How much time does it take?

One weekly group call (60-90 minutes). The monthly module at your own pace. The work between calls is your real business, not homework. If you have 2-3 hours per week to put toward the business side of the company, Roofing School fits.

Is there a trial period?

Yes. 14 days to see the inside before you commit to anything. If the content, the calls, and the community are not worth $497 a month to your business, cancel before day 14. No friction, no questions.

How is this different from other roofing business education?

Roofing School covers all four pillars: accounting, marketing, production, and admin. It is not a sales-tactics program, not a storm-chasing system, and not a D2D closer course. It is full-stack roofing business education for the company owner who wants to understand and run every part of the operation, not just one lane.

What does it cost?

Roofing School is a monthly membership. Pricing surfaces on the join page. The 14-day trial is available so you can evaluate the content and the calls before the first full billing cycle. No explicit pricing in this sample; surfaces on the join flow.

What if I want more than the group calls?

Roofing School is the membership tier. If you want direct one-on-one time with Dmitry, that route is the 30-minute or 60-minute consulting call on dmitrylipinskiy.com. If you want a live event format, the 2-day workshop runs separately. Roofing School feeds both; it does not replace either.

What about Directorii?

Directorii is the contractor directory and homeowner-guarantee SaaS product. It is a separate platform from Roofing School. Some Roofing School members also use Directorii as the verified-contractor credential for their business. The two products are independent; Roofing School does not require a Directorii subscription.
