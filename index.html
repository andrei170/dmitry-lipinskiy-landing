The Four Pillars

Four pillars. Every roofing company runs on these.

Accounting and job costing. Marketing and lead generation. Production and operations. Hiring and team building.

Pillar One · Accounting And Job Costing

Know your gross margin per job. Not the number you guess, the number in QuickBooks.

By the end of the pillar, your job costing is documented, your margin per service line is tracked, and the overhead allocation is locked.

The single biggest reason a busy roofing company is broke is the job costing is off and the overhead is not tracked. Roofing School walks the QuickBooks setup, the margin calculation, the labor burden rate, and the three numbers every owner should know by memory.

Pillar Two · Marketing And Lead Generation

Stop being referral-only. Build a three-channel lead system that does not depend on word of mouth.

By the end of the pillar, the lead-source breakdown is documented, the three-channel lead system is live, and the cost per lead per channel is tracked.

Most roofing companies grow on referrals. Very few break the next revenue bracket on referrals alone. The ceiling is the referral dependency. The pillar walks the three lead channels every roofing company should run and the budget allocation per channel by company stage.

Pillar Three · Production And Operations

Stop being the bottleneck in your own company. Build SOPs that let the company run without you on every job.

By the end of the pillar, the production SOPs are documented, the daily ops cadence is locked, and the owner is removed from at least three decisions per week.

The owner who is the production manager, the salesperson, and the accountant is the ceiling. Roofing School walks the SOP framework, the daily ops cadence, and the delegation map that gets the owner out of the field-supervisor role without dropping quality.

Pillar Four · Hiring And Team Building

Build a roofing team that does not need you to babysit it.

By the end of the pillar, the recruiting funnel is live, the onboarding sequence is documented, and the retention compensation structure is reviewed.

The gap between hiring a skilled roofer and retaining them is almost always a compensation structure or culture problem. The pillar walks the recruiting funnel, the onboarding sequence, and the three non-compensation reasons roofing crews leave.

The YouTube content shows you what the four pillars are. Roofing School walks you through fixing them inside your actual business with the install package, the cohort cadence, and the visit-your-business guarantee. Free content educates. The install operates.

Is this a sales-tactics course?

No. The lane is full-stack roofing business operations: accounting, marketing, production, hiring. Sales-rep tactics, storm-chasing strategies, and door-to-door canvassing belong to a different segment of roofing education. Roofing School is for the owner who is building the business behind the trade.

How fast will I see results?

Roofing School measures install completion and operator discipline, not month-one revenue. The first measurable outcome is in week one: the job-costing audit locked. The visit-your-business guarantee is the mechanism that sits behind the install. Anyone who tells you a guaranteed-revenue outcome on a calendar in the roofing trade is selling you something else.
